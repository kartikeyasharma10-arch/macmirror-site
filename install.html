Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type “Terminal”, press Return), paste this line and press Return:

It downloads Mac Mirror, puts it in Applications and opens it. Takes about 10 seconds. See exactly what it does.

Or install the usual way

Download MacMirror.dmg

  1. Open the DMG from Downloads and drag Mac Mirror onto the Applications folder (follow the arrow).
  2. Open Applications and double-click Mac Mirror. macOS says it “could not verify” the app. That's normal for indie apps sold outside the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to “Mac Mirror was blocked”. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, then click Open Anyway again.
  4. Click Allow when Mac Mirror asks for the camera. Your video never leaves your Mac.
Don’t see “Open Anyway”?

The button only shows for about an hour after you try to open the app, so double-click Mac Mirror again first. Or skip it entirely: paste this into Terminal and press Return.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Mac Mirror.app"

You only do this once. The floating mirror then appears on the right edge of your screen.
